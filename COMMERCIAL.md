# Commercial licensing

`@open-e2ee/signal-protocol-sdk` is dual-licensed. This file explains the
commercial path; the open-source path is the
[GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later](https://github.com/open-e2ee/signal-protocol-js/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Do you need a commercial license?

You do **not** need one if your application complies with AGPLv3 — including
its network-use provision (section 13): users who interact with your service
over a network must be able to obtain the complete corresponding source of your
application under AGPLv3.

You likely **do** need one if any of these describe your product:

- Closed-source or proprietary application, mobile or web, that ships or serves
  this SDK.
- You cannot offer your application's source under AGPLv3 terms — for
  contractual, competitive, or policy reasons.
- You are embedding the SDK in a product you redistribute to customers who
  will not receive AGPLv3 source (OEM).

## What the commercial license changes

- **Grant.** The right to use, modify, and distribute the SDK in a proprietary
  product, without the AGPLv3 source-provision obligations.
- **Scope.** Licensed per product, covering `@open-e2ee/signal-protocol-sdk`
  and its subpath adapters as named in your license record.
- **Support.** Any support terms are those stated in your order form; nothing
  is implied here.

It does **not** change the software: same package, same code, same versions,
same [security policy](https://github.com/open-e2ee/signal-protocol-js/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
There is no "commercial edition."

1. Read the terms at [open-e2ee.dev/licensing](https://open-e2ee.dev/licensing).

3. Growth, Enterprise, or anything unusual (OEM, source escrow, security
   review): email [licensing@open-e2ee.dev](mailto:licensing@open-e2ee.dev).

## Evaluation

Evaluate under AGPLv3 first — the full SDK, not a trial build. Nothing about
evaluation, prototyping, or internal proof-of-concept work requires a
commercial license before you ship to users.
